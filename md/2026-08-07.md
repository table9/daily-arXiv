### Impact of spin-orbit coupling on electron correlation corrections to the density of states in anisotropic conductors

The low-energy band consists of two warped sheets weakly tunnel-coupled transversely; SOC splits the sheets into helicity branches with a fixed spin axis. Working in a Matsubara space, we compute the exchange contribution in the diffusion channel with dynamically screened Coulomb interaction and an impurity ladder. The resulting DOS anomaly exhibits a dimensional crossover governed by the transverse coupling scale $\varepsilon_c$.
