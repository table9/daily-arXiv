### Universal electrical resistivity in iron-based high-temperature superconductors
- **arXiv**: [2610.23865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23865)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23865.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Universal electrical resistivity in iron-based high-temperature superconductors
- **Authors**: E. F. Talantsev
- **Categories**: cond-mat.supr-con, cond-mat.str-el
- **TL;DR**: 作者提出"最近邻并联电阻率模型"，将面内电阻率ρ_ab(T)视为布洛赫-格林艾森通道与阿伦尼乌斯跳跃通道并联之和，成功描述多种掺杂铁基超导体的ρ_ab(T)，并据此提取德拜温度和电子-声子耦合常数。

Temperature-dependent electrical resistivity $ρ(T)$ in cuprate, iron-based, and nickelate superconductors represents one of unresolved problems in modern solid-state physics, because of the high absolute value of $ρ(T)$, which, in many cases, exceeds the Ioffe-Rigel criterion, and a large difference in the $ρ(T)$ shape for samples with different doping. Here I show that the nearest neighbors parallel resistivity model (arXiv: 2607.23484) can well describe the in-plane resistivity $ρ_{ab}(T)$ in iron-based superconductors (IBS) of type 11, 111, 122, 1111, 1144 and 42214 in which the spin density wave phase or nematic order is suppressed by doping. The model is based on the postulate that $ρ_{ab}(T)$ is a sum of two parallel conduction channels, where one channel is described by the Bloch-Grüneisen equation and the other by the Arrhenius hopping equation. Consequently, the variety of $ρ_{ab}(T)$ in the IBS is explained as a variation of the Debye temperature $Θ_D$ and the hopping activation energy $E_a$. The derived Debye temperatures were used to estimate the electron-phonon coupling constant $λ_{e-ph}$in the studied IBS.

### Resonant suppression of the $π$-type Josephson component in a Kitaev-ladder junction
- **arXiv**: [2610.23875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23875)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23875.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Resonant suppression of the $π$-type Josephson component in a Kitaev-ladder junction
- **Authors**: Cheng-Rong Xie, Hiroki Tsuchiura, Yukio Tanaka
- **Categories**: cond-mat.supr-con
- **TL;DR**: 通过正常段离散能级失谐与分支选择机制，可调控Kitaev梯超导约瑟夫森结耦合符号（0-π转变）。

We investigate Josephson transport through a finite normal segment connecting two Kitaev-ladder superconductors and show that discrete-level detuning controls the sign of the equilibrium Josephson coupling through a branch-selection mechanism. Away from resonance, detuning separates two competing low-energy branches, allowing inter-leg hybridization to clamp the $0$-phase-favoring branch while leaving the $π$-favoring branch dispersive. The resulting negative first harmonic evolves from a branch-switching response at weak coupling into a smooth $π$-junction regime. At resonance the two clamping thresholds coincide, so the finite interval in which only one branch remains dispersive disappears. Once the inter-leg coupling exceeds the resonant clamping threshold, both low-energy branches become independent of the Josephson phase. The remaining finite-energy states then generate a $0$-type Josephson coupling. At $φ=π$, the helicity decomposition yields an exact clamping identity for arbitrary finite normal-segment length. Within the controlled sweet-point finite-segment theory, serial propagation fixes the off-resonant fourth-order harmonic to be negative, whereas an isolated resonance leaves a positive residual harmonic within the resonant clamping regime. Numerical calculations demonstrate that the resonance-induced suppression persists away from the solvable phase choice. Thus normal-level resonance does not merely enhance Josephson transmission; it changes which low-energy branches contribute to the Josephson coupling.

### Multiple propagating modes in random polycrystals
- **arXiv**: [2610.24047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24047)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24047.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Multiple propagating modes in random polycrystals
- **Authors**: Ming Huang, Stanislav I. Rokhlin, Michael J. S. Lowe
- **Categories**: cond-mat.mtrl-sci, physics.app-ph
- **TL;DR**: 通过三维多晶体的晶粒尺度有限元模拟，在频率–波数平面直接观测到多散射理论预言的相干弹性波多根现象：低频单一模式在几何区演化为两个模式，而可观测模式数由衰减层级而非根的数目决定。

Multiple-scattering theory predicts that the coherent elastic wave in a statistically isotropic polycrystal is not a single wave. Its dispersion equation admits multiple roots of a given polarization, but these extra modes have never been observed. Here, using grain-scale finite-element simulations of three-dimensional polycrystals, we resolve the coherent field in the frequency--wavenumber plane and directly observe the predicted multiplicity for both transverse and longitudinal excitations: a single mode at low frequencies gives way to two in the geometric regime as the wavelength approaches the grain correlation length. It is this attenuation hierarchy, not the root count, that dictates how many modes propagate and can be observed. Coherent wave propagation in random media is thus inherently multimodal in the geometric regime, which we reach here for the first time in polycrystals.
