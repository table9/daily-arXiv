### Stochastic Diversity Stabilization in Nonlinear Competitive Dynamics: A Statistical Mechanics Approach to Fluctuation Suppression and Finite-Size Scaling
- **arXiv**: [2610.30169](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30169)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30169.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Stochastic Diversity Stabilization in Nonlinear Competitive Dynamics: A Statistical Mechanics Approach to Fluctuation Suppression and Finite-Size Scaling
- **Authors**: Ruocun Ou
- **Categories**: cond-mat.stat-mech, nlin.AO
- **TL;DR**: 研究非线性随机复制子动力学的多样性维持条件与机制，发现噪声阈值幂律指数alpha≈(γ+2)/2为模型特异的量，且(γ,σ)平面上的涨落抑制来自路径多样性的收敛而非临界性或弛豫加速。

Stochastic forcing can prevent diversity collapse in competitive dynamical systems, yet the quantitative conditions and the dynamical mechanism responsible remain poorly characterized. We address both questions in a class of nonlinear stochastic replicator dynamics on the N-dimensional probability simplex, parameterized by a nonlinearity exponent gamma and additive Gaussian noise of intensity sigma. Under the baseline interaction, the minimum noise required to sustain diversity decays as a power law, sigma_c ~ N^(-alpha); replacing the baseline interaction with a superlinear variant shifts alpha from about 1.1 to about 2.0, in agreement with a linear-stability prediction alpha = (gamma+2)/2, identifying alpha as a model-specific quantity rather than a universal index. Scanning the (gamma, sigma) plane reveals a smooth dynamical crossover: the fluctuation susceptibility chi_H = N Var(H) peaks at gamma ~ 0.7 (chi_H ~ 0.6) and is suppressed at high gamma, with no divergence near gamma ~ 1.4; matched finite-size analysis finds no systematic divergence of the peak with N, ruling out a genuine second-order phase transition. The entropy relaxation time tau_relax(gamma) remains approximately constant (about 2.9 time units) across the full range of gamma, even as chi_H decreases sixfold; this decoupling between relaxation timescale and fluctuation amplitude implies that suppression arises from convergence of diverse pathways to similar endpoints rather than from accelerated relaxation along a single channel. These findings identify nonlinear fluctuation suppression via pathway multiplicity -- distinct from both noise-induced transitions and equilibrium criticality -- as the operative stabilization pattern in this class of nonequilibrium stochastic systems.

- **Authors**: Julian Olszewski, Zijian Liu, Jie Xu
- **Categories**: cond-mat.stat-mech
- **TL;DR**: 在弱耦合可分割四元振子链中，共振近邻的能量往返交换会改变早期Green-Kubo平台；该成对热流积分可映射为经典摆的俘获响应，从而给出无需拟合参数的定量参照。

Comparisons with chaotic cells and energy-conserving noise show regimes in which coherent return is absent or interrupted over the measured windows. These finite-time results provide a quantitative reference for separating the early exchange plateau from subsequent correlated dynamics.

### Foresight-dependent entropy production in stochastic two-agent dynamics
- **arXiv**: [2610.30189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30189)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30189.pdf)

- **Authors**: Arpan Dey
- **Categories**: cond-mat.stat-mech
- **TL;DR**: 研究前瞻性优化（foresight）能否在即时协调偏好为互惠的情形下产生非互易性。在最小随机线性二次博弈中精确计算稳态概率流与熵产生率，发现互惠协调下不可逆性从τ^5阶才出现、归一化熵产生按τ^8标度；而顺从-逆反耦合下短前瞻极限非零，且先以τ^2减小。

Thus, forward-looking optimization can generate or reduce normalized stationary irreversibility, depending on the underlying interaction.

- **TL;DR**: 本文利用无限圆柱上的segment DMRG数值模拟相互作用的双量子自旋霍尔绝缘体（DQSHI）边缘，验证了field theory预言的"玻色边缘相"：单费米子激发被打开能隙而费米子对保持无能隙。

### Discrete time liquids
- **arXiv**: [2610.30266](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30266)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30266.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Discrete time liquids
- **Authors**: Zuo Wang, Paul M. Schindler, Marin Bukov, Marios H. Michael
- **Categories**: cond-mat.str-el, cond-mat.quant-gas, cond-mat.stat-mech, quant-ph
- **TL;DR**: 本文提出“离散时间液体”概念：由时间孤子（time-soliton）导致短程时间相干的新型时空序，并与时间晶体作区分。

Discrete time crystals exhibit robust subharmonic oscillations, spontaneously breaking the discrete time-translation symmetry of a periodic drive. Here we identify discrete time liquids -- a new form of spatiotemporal order characterized by short-range temporal coherence as a result of time-solitons.
