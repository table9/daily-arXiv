### Lattice Symmetry Remnants in Spatial and Temporal Entanglement of Quantum Critical Spin Chains
- **arXiv**: [2610.31676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31676)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31676.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Lattice Symmetry Remnants in Spatial and Temporal Entanglement of Quantum Critical Spin Chains
- **Authors**: Xiao-Han Yang, Rui-Zhen Huang
- **Categories**: cond-mat.str-el
- **TL;DR**: 通过比较同一临界模型在空间与时间两种Euclidean演化下的纠缠结构，揭示晶格对称性与反常信息如何残留在红外CFT的边界实现中。

Quantum critical lattice models retain universal information about their lattice symmetries beyond the bulk data of their infrared conformal field theory (CFT). We probe these lattice symmetry remnants by comparing the spatial and temporal entanglement of two Euclidean-evolved states from the same critical model. At large length scales, short-range initial states define conformal boundaries and boundary CFT (BCFT) organizes the universal low-lying entanglement spectrum. In the temporal construction, the translation invariance along Euclidean time requires a uniform state. Moreover, the time slice $δτ\rightarrow 0$ forces a split-gate basis, resulting in a new regular uniform symmetry-preserving transfer representation. For the Ising and Potts chains, onsite symmetries result in the same BCFT structure in the spatial and temporal constructions. The spin-$1/2$ XXZ chain instead realizes the Lieb--Schultz--Mattis (LSM) constraint differently in the two Euclidean quantization. A $U(1)$-preserving spatial state has a doubled unit cell and inequivalent entanglement cuts, whereas the temporal state remains uniform and alternates between translation relation related Dirichlet sectors with integer and half-integer compact-boson zero modes. Breaking $U(1)$ removes the zero mode and the alternation. Thus microscopic symmetry and anomaly data remain visible in universal entanglement by constraining the boundary realization of the infrared CFT.

- **Categories**: cond-mat.mtrl-sci, cond-mat.str-el
- **TL;DR**: 本文证明层状反铁磁中的"块状"磁序（如↑↑↓↓）在折叠铁链堆叠成准一维阵列后可产生交错磁（altermagnetism），并预测K3Fe2Se4家族是实现该机制的平台。

Our findings unify real-space block magnetism and reciprocal-space altermagnetism, establishing folded iron chains as a tunable platform for zero-moment spintronic applications.

- **Authors**: Bahruz Suleymanli, Samir Tariverdiev, B. Tanatar
- **Categories**: cond-mat.mes-hall
- **TL;DR**: We develop a unified theory of the interband optical response of a two-dimensional semiconductor with simultaneous Rashba and Dresselhaus spin-orbit couplings in electric and magnetic fields.

We develop a unified theory of the interband optical response of a two-dimensional semiconductor with simultaneous Rashba and Dresselhaus spin-orbit couplings in electric and magnetic fields. In crossed fields, the in-plane electric field generates an effective spin-orbit drift field that modifies the Landau spectrum and spinor states, while at zero magnetic field the problem maps onto Landau--Zener spin dynamics.At zero external fields, the total joint density of states above the band gap remains unchanged, while characteristic subgap structures appear. - **TL;DR**: 提出用于多组分亚弹性扩散界面流固耦合模型的"锚定HLLD"黎曼求解器，将非保守的剪应力演化纳入黎曼求解内部，与HLLC、HLL构成波分辨率递进的三层框架。

We present an HLLD Riemann solver for a multicomponent hypoelastic diffuse-interface model of compressible fluid-solid interaction, with no separately computed interface and a deviatoric stress advanced by an objective hypoelastic rate law outside conservation-law form. The difficulty specific to this model is that the stress evolution is nonconservative and, unlike the familiar volume-fraction terms of Kapila-type models, cannot be left outside the Riemann solve once the shear waves are resolved. The shear coefficient $ρ(G+τ_{xx})$ sets the impedance of the resolved shear waves and so belongs to their eigenstructure. Anchored HLLD carries it inside the solve by evaluating the HLLD fan twice at each face, anchored in turn to each adjacent cell-centered state, with every cell advancing from the flux anchored to its own state. HLL, HLLC, and anchored HLLD form a three-tier hierarchy of increasing wave resolution in one framework. We prove local structural properties: consistency at the reconstruction order, exact species-mass conservation, and tangential-momentum and energy drift vanishing under refinement at the reconstruction's face-jump order on smooth fields, with one-dimensional tests illustrating these structural properties and demonstrating first-order drift convergence on discontinuous fields. Across one-dimensional tests and two-dimensional production simulations in the open-source solver MFC, anchored HLLD resolves both the material contact and the shear waves, HLLC the contact alone, and HLL neither sharply. Where refinement studies are shown, anchored HLLD converges toward exact or semi-analytic solutions. A three-dimensional shock-ellipsoid interaction demonstrates the solver with all six stress components active.

### Coherent axion electrodynamics in OSIRIS: a counter-propagating axion-photon parametric instability
- **arXiv**: [2610.32091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32091)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32091.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Coherent axion electrodynamics in OSIRIS: a counter-propagating axion-photon parametric instability
- **Authors**: Miles Radford, Pablo Bilbao, Thomas Grismayer, Luis Silva, Robert Bingham, Subir Sarkar, Gianluca Gregori
- **Categories**: physics.comp-ph, hep-ph
- **TL;DR**: 作者在OSIRIS粒子模拟代码中加入相干轴子-电动力学模块，发现并基准测试了一种由两束等幅反向传播电磁泵驱动的参量轴子-光子不稳定性，模拟结果与Floquet解析理论吻合。

We present a coherent axion-electrodynamics extension of the OSIRIS particle-in-cell code and use it to identify and benchmark a parametric axion-photon instability supported by two equal counter-propagating electromagnetic pumps. The module evolves a cell-centered axion field together with a separately stored first-order electromagnetic response on the native staggered OSIRIS grid. Re-examining the previously proposed strictly co-linear branch shows that it is removed when the axion-Maxwell equations are reduced consistently. A closely related standing-wave geometry, however, supports a distinct Floquet instability. We derive the coupled Floquet problem for an equal-amplitude counter-propagating pump pair and obtain a reduced analytic growth rate and cutoff for the dominant long-wavelength envelope branch. For an axion mass-to-pump-frequency ratio of 0.06 and a per-beam vector-potential amplitude of 0.3, simulations at coupling strengths of 0.60, 0.80, and 1.00 reproduce both the growth-rate spectrum and the coupling-dependent instability boundary. The reduced theory predicts envelope-mode cutoffs of 8.44, 10.80, and 13.23, while OSIRIS finds exponential growth through modes 8, 10, and 13, respectively, with the next resolved modes stable. The electromagnetic diagnostics additionally resolve exponentially growing sidebands at twice the pump wavenumber, while the zero-wavenumber electric component exhibits no sustained growth, as expected for the unstable Floquet eigenvector. The agreement provides an end-to-end benchmark of the coherent axion-electromagnetic coupling and demonstrates that parametric growth of the axion field is present in a two-beam pump geometry.

### CoPiT: A conditional Position-induced Transformer for aerodynamic field prediction on unstructured meshes
- **arXiv**: [2610.32299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32299)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32299.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: CoPiT: A conditional Position-induced Transformer for aerodynamic field prediction on unstructured meshes
- **Authors**: Junfeng Chen, Yongchen Fan, Zhonghua Qiao, Kailiang Wu
- **Categories**: physics.comp-ph, cs.LG, math.NA, physics.flu-dyn
- **TL;DR**: 提出CoPiT——以Position-induced Transformer为几何骨干，通过多级FiLM注入全局条件向量的神经算子，用于非结构网格RANS气动场快速代理预测。

High-fidelity Reynolds-averaged Navier--Stokes (RANS) simulations remain a standard tool for aerodynamic analysis, yet each solve on an unstructured mesh is computationally expensive, making many-query tasks such as design exploration, optimization, and multi-condition evaluation costly. Neural operators provide mesh-flexible surrogates for mapping geometry and operating conditions to flow fields; however, in many existing architectures, global parameters such as the freestream state and design variables are concatenated with pointwise inputs, leaving their influence on intermediate features implicit. This paper proposes CoPiT, a \textbf{Co}nditional \textbf{P}osition-induced \textbf{T}ransformer that builds on the Position-induced Transformer (PiT) as its geometric backbone and injects a global condition vector through multi-stage feature-wise linear modulation (FiLM), thereby making parametric modulation explicit while preserving geometry-based attention weights. The model is evaluated on two-dimensional AirfRANS airfoil fields, including scarce-data and extrapolation regimes, and on three-dimensional BlendedNet++ blended-wing-body surfaces. On the AirfRANS full-data test set, CoPiT reduces surface and volume losses by approximately 54\% and 24\% relative to Transolver, respectively, and achieves the lowest overall normalized test MSE among the compared baselines on BlendedNet++. Matched ablations show that FiLM conditioning generally improves upon input concatenation, while the preferred placement is task-dependent. CoPiT thus provides a practical operator-learning surrogate for parametric aerodynamics on unstructured meshes.
