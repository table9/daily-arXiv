### The Ising lattice gas and the surface diffusion of CO on Cu(001) at finite coverage
- **arXiv**: [2610.33147](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33147)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.33147.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: The Ising lattice gas and the surface diffusion of CO on Cu(001) at finite coverage
- **Authors**: S. Miret-Artés
- **Categories**: cond-mat.stat-mech
- **TL;DR**: 将晶格气体ISF记忆方程用于Kawasaki动力学下的Ising晶格气体，得到闭式静态结构因子与有效跳跃率，揭示单指数下界仅弱耦合时成立，并在CO/Cu(001)中拟合出≥35-50 meV的近邻排斥。

The memory equation of the coherent intermediate scattering function (ISF), derived previously for lattice gases, is applied to the Ising lattice gas under Kawasaki dynamics. The ISF follows the decay of a density wave of adparticles, a superposition of exponential relaxation modes: the decay starts at a rate fixed by a sum rule and is then slowed by a memory. For CO/Cu(001), the lattice gas reproduces the dephasing rates measured along two crystal directions and requires a nearest-neighbour repulsion of at least $35$--$50$~meV, whatever the jump rates; the measured rate is then close to the inverse correlation time, and the memory is predicted to grow with coverage.
