### Mpemba effect as an entropy-production race:Finite-amplitude bounds on crossing times in Markovian relaxation
- **arXiv**: [2610.40704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40704)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40704.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Mpemba effect as an entropy-production race:Finite-amplitude bounds on crossing times in Markovian relaxation
- **Authors**: Mesfin Taye
- **Categories**: cond-mat.stat-mech
- **TL;DR**: 该文将Mpemba效应表述为"熵产生竞赛"，通过对归一化熵产生率差的累积积分给出相对熵首次交叉时间的上下界，并在三态可逆模型中把交叉时间锁定在0.1659至0.1674之间。

Two canonical preparations evolving under a common final Markov generator can exhibit the Mpemba effect because they excite different relaxation modes. We bound the first crossing of their relative entropies by treating relaxation as an entropy-production race. The exact balance $-\dot D=\dot e_p$ implies that a meeting occurs when the accumulated \emph{difference} of normalized entropy-production rates, $\int_0^t(r_H-r_C)\,\mathrm ds$ with $r_α=\dot e_p^α/D_α$, equals the initial logarithmic deficit $\ln[D_H(0)/D_C(0)]$. Global entropy-decay inequalities yield a lower crossing-time bound when the entropy-decay constant is bounded from below and the hot rate from above; we supply both certificates. A nonzero cubic moment of a slowest eigenfunction is sufficient for the modified logarithmic Sobolev constant to lie strictly below the spectral gap. Combining established relative-entropy comparison inequalities with Markov contraction and the actual mode amplitudes gives finite-amplitude crossing bounds without a small-deviation expansion. For an exactly strong, reversible three-state preparation these give $0.1659\le t_M\le0.1674$ in model time units, independently certified by outward-rounded interval arithmetic. This interval is narrower than $1\%$ and contains $t_M=0.1662209$. The conservative global-rate comparison used here gives only $t_M\ge0.042$. The hotter preparation starts $6.12$ times farther from equilibrium, yet it reaches $D=10^{-5}$ at $t=0.233$, compared with $t=0.844$ for the colder one. Mean energy crosses later than relative entropy. For a fixed driven generator, nonadiabatic entropy production governs the same balance. These results concern finite-state relaxation in a specified distance and do not predict water-freezing times.

### SKFitter: Symmetry resolved Slater Koster tight-binding models from first principles for s, p, d and f electrons, spin orbit coupling and magnetism
- **arXiv**: [2610.40729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40729)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40729.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: SKFitter: Symmetry resolved Slater Koster tight-binding models from first principles for s, p, d and f electrons, spin orbit coupling and magnetism
- **Authors**: Sundeep Kapila, Bhaskaran Muralidharan, Satadeep Bhattacharjee
- **Categories**: cond-mat.mtrl-sci, cs.MS, math-ph
- **TL;DR**: SKFitter是一个开源Python框架，可从DFT（QE/VASP）结果构建并优化对称分辨的Slater-Koster紧束缚模型，支持s-f轨道、SOC、磁性交换及非正交基，在8种代表性材料上实现9-41 meV能带误差并能复现AHC/SHC等响应。

Constructing accurate and physically interpretable tight binding (TB) Hamiltonians from first principles calculations remains challenging, particularly for materials exhibiting complex orbital hybridization, spin orbit coupling (SOC), and magnetic order. SKFitter is an open-source Python framework for constructing and optimizing symmetry resolved Slater Koster TB models directly from density functional theory (DFT) calculations performed using Quantum ESPRESSO or VASP. The framework supports s to f orbitals, incorporates on site SOC and site resolved magnetic exchange interactions, and accommodates both orthogonal and nonorthogonal basis. A central feature is the symmetry resolved parameterization of orbital manifolds, which allows independent two centre hopping integrals between symmetry allowed orbital groups and across distinct neighbour shells. SKFitter also identifies missing orbital contributions and guide systematic basis set refinement and optimizes parameters through weights. The resulting Hamiltonian can be used to evaluate anomalous (AHC) and spin Hall conductivities (SHC), and topological invariants. We demonstrate the framework using eight representative materials spanning semimetals, semiconductors, transition metals, f-electron compounds, and magnetic systems. Symmetry resolved models achieve band energy errors of 9 to 41 meV within 1 eV of the Fermi level. The fitted Hamiltonians reproduce characteristic features of the SHC response of Pt, the SOC dependence of the AHC of Fe, and the momentum dependent altermagnetic spin splitting of MnTe without any altermagnet specific interaction. For EuMn2Bi2, projection guided orbital refinement reduces the near Fermi level fitting error from 120 to 32 meV. Together, these results establish SKFitter as a flexible and reproducible framework for developing symmetry consistent, physically interpretable TB models of complex materials.

- **TL;DR**: 提出动量加权界面稳定化（MWIS），通过在流体子问题界面压力块上添加由流体与结构界面动量算子构造的一致性算子，缓解强附加质量下的分区FSI不稳定。

Partitioned fluid-structure interaction (FSI) schemes permit the reuse of optimized standalone solvers but can become unstable under strong fluid added-mass effects, particularly when structural inertia is small relative to fluid inertia. This work traces the instability to the discrepancy between the interface pressure Schur complements of the monolithic and Dirichlet- Neumann partitioned systems, as revealed by a fixed-point analysis of the linearized FSI problem. Motivated by this analysis, a momentum-weighted interface stabilization (MWIS) is proposed, in which the interface pressure block of the fluid subproblem is augmented by an operator constructed from the fluid and structural interface momentum operators. Numerical experiments, spanning a linear model and established nonlinear FSI benchmarks, demonstrate improved coupling robustness in regimes of strong added-mass where the standard Dirichlet-Neumann scheme diverges.
