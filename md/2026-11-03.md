### Optical Signature of Bias-Induced Carrier Injection in a WSe2/MoS2 Heterostructure Diode

More broadly, this approach establishes spatially resolved PL spectroscopy as a versatile tool for pinpointing the interfaces governing device operation in van der Waals heterostructures.

- **Categories**: cond-mat.stat-mech
- **TL;DR**: 该论文研究三角晶格Ising反铁磁体在L×L环面上、奇尺寸L下受次近邻反铁磁相互作用选择时基态的分类与熵，并给出周期自旋基态与蜂窝二聚体覆盖的有限尺寸熵密度双侧界。
