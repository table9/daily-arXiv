### Quasicrystals are Functionally Graded Materials
- **arXiv**: [2611.05478](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05478)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05478.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Quasicrystals are Functionally Graded Materials
- **Authors**: Michael J. Landry, Dominic Bollettieri, Akhil Sadam, Denisse Córdova Carrizales, Mingda Li
- **Categories**: cond-mat.mtrl-sci
- **TL;DR**: 本文证明准晶体可精确表示为一个带有空间梯度相位子(phason)的周期近似体，从而把准晶体与功能梯度材料联系起来，并据此建立一套可控的准晶量子理论。

Quasicrystals exhibit long-range order without translational periodicity, posing challenges to compute their electronic properties. Here we show that a quasicrystal can be represented exactly as a periodic approximant with a spatially graded phason, establishing a connection to functionally graded materials. Building on this connection, we develop a controlled quantum theory of quasicrystals. We derive a state-dependent control parameter governed by the quantum metric of local Bloch states, determining when finite approximations are reliable. We further construct a Hamiltonian that correctly predicts Bragg gap positions, quasimomentum equivalence, and quasiperiodic eigenstates to all orders in phason strain within a finite Fourier window. We verify the theory numerically and propose a diffraction-based experimental test of the gap shift induced by phason strain. Our framework opens a route to predicting the electronic structure and functional properties of quasicrystals using methods developed for functionally graded materials.

### Resonantly interacting $2e/3$ anyons: a controlled microscopic theory of anyon superconductivity
- **arXiv**: [2611.05529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05529)  ·  [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05529.pdf)
- **Title (EN)**: Resonantly interacting $2e/3$ anyons: a controlled microscopic theory of anyon superconductivity
- **Authors**: Qingchen Li, Pavel A. Nosov, Zhaoyu Han, Si-Yuan Chen, Taige Wang, Eslam Khalaf
- **Categories**: cond-mat.str-el, cond-mat.supr-con
- **TL;DR**: 本文建立轻掺杂Laughlin态中任意子超导的微观理论，将电子相互作用与共振任意子气的普适物理联系起来，并围绕两体束缚共振发现由Aharonov-Casher型任意子哈密顿量控制的精确可解固定点。

We develop a microscopic theory of anyon superconductivity that connects electronic interactions in a lightly doped Laughlin state to the universal physics of a resonantly interacting anyon gas. Using geometric quantization of Laughlin quasiholes, we derive the effective many-body Hamiltonian of dilute charge-$2e/3$ anyons, including their valley structure, dispersion, and statistical and short-range interactions, together with an explicit map between anyon and electron observables. This reduces the dilute many-body problem to a few parameters fixed by data computable from few anyon numerics. In analogy with resonant Fermi and Bose gases, we organize the dilute anyon problem around a two-body binding resonance and its relevant and marginal perturbations. For a three-valley gas of charge-$2e/3$ anyons, we identify a resonance whose long-distance theory is governed by an Aharonov-Casher anyon Hamiltonian whose magnetic field is the statistical Chern-Simons field and whose ground state is exactly solvable. Using the ground state wavefunction, we demonstrate Cooper-pair off-diagonal long-range order directly in electron coordinates and determine the pairing symmetry and vortex-lattice structure. The leading perturbations at the fixed point are a relevant pair detuning and a marginal three-body contact in the valley-singlet channel, which determine the compressibility and sound velocity. A repulsive three-body coupling is marginally irrelevant, allowing the fixed point to control a parametrically broad dilute regime near pair resonance, while attractive three-body interactions instead generate charge-$2e$ trimers. More broadly, our results show that binding thresholds between competing anyon composites host universal resonant regimes that are not captured by the lowest-energy anyon species alone, providing a new setting for controlled theories of anyon matter.

- **Categories**: cond-mat.supr-con, cond-mat.mtrl-sci, quant-ph

Our results establish horizontal mirror symmetry as an exact and practical filter of e-ph scattering processes.

We find that accurate energetics are obtained for states dominated by open-shell character, and that for certain atoms and diatomics the accuracy can be significantly improved when the trial is made to also preserve the relevant angular momentum symmetries. Furthermore, our study reveals that the open-shell CSF trial is suboptimal when the target electronic state reflects a significant mixture of ionic and covalent character, e.g., at shorter bond lengths in H-He-H and for the superexchange-stabilized ground state of [2Fe-2S]$^{2+}$. Our findings suggest that ph-AFQMC using CSF trial states is a promising method for many (but not all) strongly correlated molecular states, especially given its relatively low computational cost, and that it provides a reasonable non-perturbative way to recover some of the dynamic correlation that is otherwise missing in the CSF.

### Discovering like a scientist: Literature-informed dimensionless learning for physical closure of data-driven equations

- **TL;DR**: 提出知识引导的无量纲学习框架π-former，从1288个已有无量纲组中学习变量关联与指数分配模式，在精确量纲约束下自动发现无量纲坐标，并与方程发现结合，实现跨工况的物理尺度规律恢复与实验泛化。

Data-driven methods can recover compact equations, yet the physical dependence of their coefficients across operating conditions often remains unresolved.
